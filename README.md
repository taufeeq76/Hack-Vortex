# 💪 VYLO — Your Fitness. Your Growth. Your Journey.

**VYLO** is an AI-powered student fitness platform designed to help college students build healthier lifestyles through personalized workouts, nutrition guidance, hydration tracking, sleep monitoring, and campus fitness challenges.

VYLO makes fitness more accessible by adapting recommendations to students' daily routines, activity levels, goals, and available resources.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

College students often struggle to maintain consistent fitness routines because of busy academic schedules, limited access to fitness resources, and a lack of personalized guidance. VYLO aims to make fitness simpler, more engaging, and easier to maintain throughout college life.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🏋️ **Personalized Fitness Plans** — Get fitness goals and workout recommendations tailored to your routine and energy levels.
- 🏠 **No-Equipment Workouts** — Exercise anywhere with accessible workout options.
- 💧 **Hydration Tracking** — Monitor daily water intake and build healthy habits.
- 😴 **Sleep Monitoring** — Track sleep habits and support better recovery.
- 🍎 **ZYRO AI Nutrition Assistant** — Analyze food images to estimate calories, protein, and other nutrients, where supported by the configured AI service.
- 😊 **Mood-to-Move AI** — Discover workout suggestions based on your mood and energy level.
- 🏆 **Campus Challenges & Leaderboards** — Stay motivated through fitness challenges and friendly competition.
- 🗺️ **Campus Fitness Map** — Discover available fitness spaces and workout locations around campus.
- 🤝 **Squad Ascend** — Stay accountable and motivate friends through shared fitness goals.
- 📈 **FitScore** — Track your fitness progress through a unified progress indicator.

## 🚀 Our Fitness Journey

VYLO supports three stages of personal growth:

- **THRIVE** — Build healthy habits and establish a fitness foundation.
- **ASCEND** — Improve consistency, strength, and endurance.
- **EVOLVE** — Challenge yourself and continue progressing toward your goals.
